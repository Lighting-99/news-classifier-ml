Asus has carved out its space in the laptop segment over the years, especially with its ExpertBook line. It has consistently ranked high on dependability, usability, and performance. Taking that approach a notch higher, Asus has launched the ExpertBook Ultra in the premium segment.

Starting at ₹2,39,990, the ExpertBook Ultra promises to deliver across parameters. It targets high-profile business users who want power and portability in one device. It is also among the first laptops to feature Intel’s Panther Lake processors, unveiled at CES 2026. That alone puts it in an interesting position when pitted against the Apple MacBook Pro and the Samsung Galaxy Book 6 Pro.

Let’s find out if it lives up to the promise.

Design

The review unit of the Asus ExpertBook Ultra comes in the Morn Grey colour, and it immediately stands out, looking premium without trying too hard. The finish feels clean and minimalistic, and the branding is subtle. Asus has used a nano-ceramic coating that keeps the surface smudge-resistant even after long use. Underneath, the build quality backs up the design. The chassis uses aerospace-grade magnesium-aluminium alloy, which keeps it both light-weight and durable. It feels sturdy and well put together, while the 9H durability adds an extra layer of confidence for long-term use.

One subtle but standout touch is the ExpertLumi dynamic lighting. It softly lights up during boot, login, and shutdown, lending the device a stylish feel without being distracting. It is a small detail, but it gives the laptop a distinct identity. At around 0.99 kg and 10.9 mm thickness, this is built for mobility. Despite the slim profile, Asus has included a full set of ports, including Thunderbolt 4, USB-A, HDMI, and a headphone jack. It is a practical approach that many premium laptops miss.

Display

The ExpertBook Ultra features a 3K tandem OLED display—and it delivers on clarity and colour accuracy. With 100% DCI-P3 coverage and Delta-E < 1, visuals look sharp and precise. The variable refresh rate between 30Hz and 120Hz also helps keep things smooth. 

Performance

Performance is easily the biggest strength of the ExpertBook Ultra. It runs on the Intel Core Ultra X7 358H processor with integrated Arc graphics. This is paired with up to 64 GB RAM and 1 TB SSD, making it capable of handling demanding workloads.

From multi-tasking and editing to video calls and heavy office work, everything runs smoothly. The laptop feels fast and reliable, even under pressure. Thermal performance is also strong, thanks to the advanced cooling system that supports up to 50W performance in turbo mode.
