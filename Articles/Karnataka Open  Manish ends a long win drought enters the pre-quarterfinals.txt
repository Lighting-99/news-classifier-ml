Manish Sureshkumar’s last win in an ATP Challenger tournament match came in November 2019. A relieved Manish managed to break the long drought in the first round of the Karnataka Open ATP Challenger here on Tuesday with a 6-4, 7-6(7) win over American qualifier Ronit Karki.

Manish (World No. 829) stayed consistent with his groundstrokes to outclass Karki, the 2025 Wimbledon junior finalist.

“Normally I like to play long rallies, but here that it not possible because the ball travels faster and longer. Having good ball control really helps in these conditions,” the 26-year-old Manish said.

In another round-of-32 clash, Japan’s Kokoro Isomura defeated an inconsistent Karan Singh 7-6(6), 6-3. Karan made far too away unforced errors, and was tentative with his volleys. Playing in the familiar setting of his home stadium, 19-year-old Kriish Tyagi took out Japanese qualifier Taiyo Yamanaka 6-2, 7-5.
